The children were running through the gardens when their teacher arrived. She was carrying several books and asked whether they had finished their studies. One student said the new lessons were better than the earlier exercises, while another explained that he went home feeling happier after practising. Their feet were tired, but they kept walking because the weather was pleasant. Later, the class discussed how machines are learning from examples and why language researchers compare different methods. The students wrote summaries, examined confusing words, and carefully checked their results. They discovered that a useful system needs accurate data, meaningful evaluation, and clear explanations rather than only faster calculations. Finally, everyone agreed to continue working together during the following week.
